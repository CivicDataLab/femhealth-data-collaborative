# National Mental Health Survey (NMHS) — Phase 1 Fact Sheets

State-level mental health infrastructure, workforce, and outcome indicators for Assam (one of the 12 states covered in NMHS Phase 1).

## Registry metadata

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | National Mental Health Survey |
| Source | https://indianmhs.nimhans.ac.in/phase1/fact-sheets |
| Publisher | National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro-Sciences (NIMHANS), Bengaluru |
| Theme | Health; Mental Health |
| Spatial coverage | India (12 states); this file is Assam |
| Temporal coverage | 2016 |
| Format | PDF (source); extracted here as a fact sheet workbook |

Indicators tracked: female population, sex ratio, female literacy rate, Community/Primary Health Centres, Sub Centres, district hospitals, registered nurses & midwives, ANMs, LHVs, ASHA/USHA workers, District Mental Health Programme (DMHP) coverage, mental hospitals, medical colleges with psychiatry departments, general hospitals with psychiatry units, mental health inpatient beds, psychiatrists, clinical psychologists, psychiatric nurses/social workers, mental health professional availability, mental health budget allocation, suicide rate, prevalence of common mental disorders, treatment gap.

## Contents

**`national_mental_health_survey.xlsx`**

- `Characterstics` — demographic and administrative/economic characteristics (population, number of districts, etc.)
- `General Facility` — general health care facility counts and availability per 1,00,000 population, Public vs. Private sector
- `HR count` — human resources in general health care facilities (specialists, availability per 1,00,000 population)
- `District` — District Mental Health Programme (DMHP) coverage indicators
- `Mental Facility` — mental-health-specific facility counts
- `Suicide Rate` — suicide rate indicators
- `Treatment Gap` — treatment gap for common mental disorders
