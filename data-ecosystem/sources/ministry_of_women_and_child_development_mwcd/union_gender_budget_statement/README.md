# Union Gender Budget Statement

Union Budget "Statement 13" — expenditure allocations for schemes benefiting women and girls, by year.

## Contents

**`gender_budget_statement_2022_2027.xlsx`** — one sheet per budget year (`2026-27`, `2025-26`, `2024-27`, `2023-24`, `2022-2023`), each an "Expenditure Profile (In ₹ Crores)" with three parts:

- **Part A** — schemes with 100% budget provision for women and girls
- **Part B** — schemes with 30–99% allocation for women and girls
- **Part C** — schemes with allocation for women and girls below 30% of the provision
