## Contents

**`cnns_factsheet_assam.xlsx`**

- `About CNNS` — survey overview
- `Under 5 Anthropometric` — key anthropometric indicators for children under 5
- `Age 5-9 Antropometric` — key anthropometric indicators for children aged 5–9
- `Adolesents Aged 10-19 Antropome` — anthropometric indicators (incl. BMI-for-age z-score) for adolescents 10–19
- `Micronutrient Deficiencies` — micronutrient deficiency prevalence
- `Non-Communicable Disease Risk` — NCD risk factor indicators

Values are percentages unless noted; anthropometric SD values are relative to WHO growth standards.
