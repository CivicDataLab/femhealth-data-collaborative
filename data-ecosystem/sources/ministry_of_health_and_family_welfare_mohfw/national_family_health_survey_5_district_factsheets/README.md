# National Family Health Survey-5 (NFHS-5) — District Factsheets

District-level factsheet indicators from NFHS-5, filtered to Assam's districts.

The source dataset covers all districts of India — this collaborative filtered it down to Assam's districts only.

## Contents

**`nfhs_assam_2019_2021.xlsx`**

- `Data` — one row per Assam district (Kokrajhar, Goalpara, …) with survey counts and headline indicators (households surveyed, women/men interviewed, female literacy, sex ratio, population under 15, etc.)
- `Data2` — state-level indicators broken out by Urban / Rural / Total, with the equivalent NFHS-4 value alongside each NFHS-5 value for comparison
- `Details` — fieldwork notes (conducted 17 Jun–21 Dec 2019 by Nielsen India Pvt. Ltd.; 30,119 households, 34,979 women, 4,973 men) and links to the source DHS Program publications
