# NCAER Survey (DES Study) — Women's Condition in Assam

Survey of women's conditions in Assam, focused on health & nutrition, educational attainment, skills/employability, and incidence of crimes against women. Commissioned by the Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES), Government of Assam.

## Contents

**`women_condition_report_2025_03_27_new.pdf`** (188 pages) — full report, "Women's Conditions in Assam," January 2025. Covers health & nutrition conditions, educational attainment, skills and employability, and incidence of crimes against women.

**`women_condition_in_assam.xlsx`** — extracted district-level indicator tables from the report:

- `Place of Birth in All Districts` — % of births by Govt. Hospital / Home / Private Hospital, per district
- `Gender-Wise Prevalece of Differ` — disease prevalence (%) by age group (<18, 18–29, 30–49, 50–64) and gender
- `Prevalence of Anaemia among Wom` — anaemia prevalence (%) among women aged 15–49, by district, split by pregnant/non-pregnant and by age band
- ` Antenatal Check-up in Assam an` — % distribution of antenatal check-up frequency (Never / One–Five+ times), per district
